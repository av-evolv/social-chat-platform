# Brand explorations

## Shared strategy

A private place for your people, their plans and the things you did together. Lead with belonging and real life. Conversation is the entry point; events and memories stay connected to it. Avoid productivity language, popularity metrics and promises of encryption before the implementation has been verified.

All four concepts use the working name **Along** so that visual design can be compared independently of naming. The prototype lets you try all five names. Names are creative candidates only; domain, app-store and trademark availability have not been checked.

## Five name candidates

| Name | Idea | Suggested line | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Along | Being included in the ordinary moments of life. | Life happens together. | A common word that would need a distinctive identity. |
| Folk | Familiar people and a sense of belonging. | A little closer to your people. | Broad meaning; a cultural or music association is possible. |
| Keeps | Shared moments worth holding on to. | Make plans. Keep the moments. | Emphasises memories more than messaging. |
| Sunday | Unhurried time spent in good company. | More time for your people. | Can imply a weekend-only product. |
| Sideby | A coined, compact expression of side by side. | Your people, by your side. | Requires pronunciation and spelling validation. |

My starting recommendation is Along with Good Company. It supports chat, plans and memories without tying the product to one feature. Close Knit is the strongest alternative if the priority is a bright, instantly recognisable messenger identity.

## 01 · Good Company

**Character:** human, thoughtful, familiar. A digital dinner table.

**Logo:** two overlapping rings and a small conversational tail. An open, lowercase wordmark. Use the editable `assets/good-company.svg` as a concept mark; final optical refinement follows selection.

**Palette:** Paper #F6F1E7; Ink #342B26; Clay #B5462D; Olive #6A7144; Peach #E9BDA5. Clay is the primary action; olive is a supporting accent. Paper and ink carry most of the interface.

**Typography:** Georgia for brand headlines and editorial memory titles; system sans for messages, controls and dates. Display 48–76px, section titles 28–36px, UI 14–16px, captions 12px. Keep serif out of dense chat.

**Elements:** 20px cards, 16px bubbles, fine warm borders, quiet shadows, irregular overlapping ring illustrations, 1.75px round-ended icons. Photography: candid tables, walks and trips; warm natural light and unobtrusive crops.

**Voice:** inviting and conversational. “Anyone up for Saturday?” / “A good day, all in one place.” / Empty state: “Your next good plan starts here.”

**Motion:** soft 180ms fades and 4px lifts. No bouncing on new messages.

**Best fit:** a lasting home for small groups and their shared history. Watch that the editorial details never slow down everyday messaging.

## 02 · Close Knit

**Character:** clear, lively, approachable. A familiar messenger with a strong graphic identity.

**Logo:** two linked, rounded speech shapes. Heavy lowercase wordmark with tight spacing.

**Palette:** Cloud #F1F5FF; Ink #152648; Cobalt #2851D8; Sky #B8D6F4; Butter #F2DE88. Cobalt anchors actions and the app icon; sky and butter are expressive surfaces.

**Typography:** system sans throughout. Heavy display weights (750–850), medium UI labels, normal message text. Display 48–76px, headings 28–36px, UI 14–16px, captions 12px.

**Elements:** 24px cards, 20px bubbles, playful interlocking tiles, high-contrast primary buttons, 2px rounded icons. Photography: honest daylight, people mid-action, blue skies and vivid details.

**Voice:** short and upbeat. “Good plan. Who’s in?” / “All your people. Right here.” / Empty state: “Get something going.”

**Motion:** 160ms transitions with a small icon response; preserve immediate message feedback.

**Best fit:** a recognisable everyday messenger that scales comfortably across web and mobile. Keep the expressive shapes outside dense conversation surfaces.

## 03 · After Hours

**Character:** expressive, intimate, creative. The group chat that becomes a night to remember.

**Logo:** four rounded loops around a shared centre, making a conversational flower. Italic serif wordmark.

**Palette:** Lilac paper #F2ECF5; Plum #44284C; Orchid #D2AFE2; Acid #E0EB96; Pink #EABECF. Plum is primary text and action. Acid highlights special moments; it is not a text colour on light backgrounds.

**Typography:** italic Georgia for brand statements and memory covers; system sans for all functional UI. Display 48–76px, headings 28–36px, UI 14–16px, captions 12px.

**Elements:** 22px cards, 18px bubbles, clover motifs, occasional tilted photo frames, 1.75px rounded icons. Photography: dusk, dinners, flash-lit moments and close crops. UI remains light and readable.

**Voice:** expressive without forced slang. “The plan starts here.” / “For the nights you’ll talk about.” / Empty state: “Something good is brewing.”

**Motion:** 200ms gentle scale on large cards only. Avoid rotation in functional interface elements.

**Best fit:** a distinctive social identity with strong memory and invitation artwork. Keep everyday language inclusive of family groups and daytime plans.

## 04 · Open Air

**Character:** grounded, relaxed, uncomplicated. Less organising; more getting together.

**Logo:** two continuous curved paths meeting around a shared centre. Clean, widely spaced lowercase wordmark.

**Palette:** Mist #EDF2ED; Forest #245446; Apricot #E9B28C; Sage #B8CDB7; Chalk #FAF8F1. Forest is the primary action and type colour. Apricot adds warmth to event artwork.

**Typography:** system sans, with lighter brand headings and medium UI labels. Display 48–76px, headings 28–36px, UI 14–16px, captions 12px. Use generous leading and space.

**Elements:** 16px cards, 16px bubbles, contour-line motifs, pill filters, 1.75px rounded icons. Photography: open spaces, neighbourhood walks, picnics and everyday adventures; keep people central.

**Voice:** calm and direct. “See you out there.” / “Less planning. More being there.” / Empty state: “Make a little room for together.”

**Motion:** 180ms crossfades. Reserve movement for navigation and meaningful state changes.

**Best fit:** an understated home for real-world friendships. Balance outdoor imagery with indoor life so it does not read as an activity tracker.

## Shared design foundations

- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px. Product screens use a 16px base gutter.
- Primary product tap targets: at least 44×44px. Keep visible keyboard focus. Use labels with icons.
- Primary UI text: 16px in the production product; compact mockups are scaled illustrations of this system.
- Chat is the default view. Plans and Memories are alternate views of shared content.
- Use the same event title and identifiers across views. Show the audience explicitly; circle labels must not imply access to every related conversation.
- Primary actions use light text on each kit’s dark accent. Standard body text uses ink on paper. Verify every new text/surface pair against WCAG AA; decorative palette swatches are not pre-approved text colours.
- Error and success states always include words and an icon, never colour alone. Final semantic colour choices should be validated against the selected kit.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences. No autoplay video or attention-seeking unread animations.
- Prototype dark lockups demonstrate brand flexibility; a complete dark UI theme remains part of the selected kit’s refinement.

## Selection and refinement

Choose a visual direction and a name independently. The next design pass can refine the selected vector mark, font family, final light/dark semantic tokens, onboarding, chat, event details, calendar, memories and invitation screens. These boards are concept kits, not a completed production design system.
